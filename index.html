How This Course Works

Course Flow

Each week you focus on one ileke: colors, function, etiquette, permissions, and care. You complete the weekly reflection and a small self-check. This course is educational (lineage patterns remain house-specific).

Teaching disclaimer: images are for identification and study. Consecration protocols, bead counts, and house patterns are not displayed.
Weekly Lessons
Week 1 — Èṣù / Eshu (Elegba) Red & Black • Roads, openings, alignment Start here
Week 2 — Obatala White • Clarity, ethics, coolness Purity & restraint
Week 3 — Ọ̀ṣun / Oshun Yellow/Gold • Sweetness, abundance Ase of attraction
Week 4 — Yemọja / Yemaya Blue & White • Protection, motherhood Emotional steadiness
Week 5 — Ṣàngó / Shango Red & White • Justice, authority Right action
Week 6 — Ògún / Ogun Green & Black • Work, iron, discipline Commitment
Week 7 — Ọya / Oya Purple/Brown • Change, ancestors Transformation
Week 8 — Òrúnmìlà / Orunmila (Ifá) Green & Yellow • Wisdom, destiny Authorization emphasis
Requirements

Weekly Requirements

  • Study the week’s ileke image + meaning
  • Complete the weekly reflection (download/copy)
  • Pass the quick self-check (checkboxes)
  • Optional: submit to SGI review
Course outcome: you can identify common ilekes, explain ethics/authority, and demonstrate correct handling.
Suggested Review

Review Rhythm

  • Day 1–2: meaning + etiquette
  • Day 3–4: permissions + care
  • Day 5: personal reflection
  • Day 6–7: recap + quiz self-check
