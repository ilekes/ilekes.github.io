How This Course Works

A public devotion + character curriculum (SGI style)
Three Pillars
  • Auset: devotion that refines intuition (the “inner knowing”).
  • Ileke: visible commitment to ethical conduct (behavior alignment).
  • Eshu: gatekeeper of speech, roads, consequences (accountability).
Public-Safe Practices
  • Breath, stillness, journaling, and ethical vows.
  • No offerings, no ritual instructions, no “secret” formulas.
  • Focus on character, clarity, and responsible speech/action.
Eshu Principle (Governs ALL 8 Weeks)

Eshu is included as the gatekeeper — meaning each week is measured by: truthful speech, clear intention, and acceptance of consequence. In public format: this becomes an ethics vow and a communication discipline.

The 8 Weeks

Auset principles correlated to Ileke themes (with Eshu governing movement)
1
Intuitional Wisdom
Auset: Inner knowing Ileke: Orunmila (destiny) Eshu: message clarity
Public Focus
  • Theme: strengthen inner guidance (without rushing).
  • Color inspiration: green / yellow (growth + illumination).
  • Body relation: “Ori” (inner head) / attention / stillness.
Daily Practice (10–15 min)
  • Quiet breathing + one question: “What truth am I avoiding?”
  • Write 5 lines: what your best self would do today.
  • One intentional decision (no impulsive action).
Eshu Accountability
  • Speak only what you can stand behind.
  • No exaggeration, no “testing people,” no baiting.
  • Observe consequence without blaming others.
2
Devotional Discipline
Auset: purification Ileke: Obatala (clarity) Eshu: sincerity test
Public Focus
  • Theme: simplify life to hear spirit more clearly.
  • Color inspiration: white (clean conduct).
  • Season: winter energy (inner refinement).
Daily Practice
  • Declutter one thing (space, schedule, or mind).
  • Silence window: 20 minutes/day (no media).
  • Write: “What habit weakens my character?”
Eshu Accountability
  • Do not pretend to be “pure.” Be honest and steady.
  • Speak less. Keep promises.
  • Let actions prove devotion.
3
Sacred Speech
Auset: power of word Ileke: Oshun (sweetness) Eshu: consequence of speech
Public Focus
  • Theme: your words build or break reality.
  • Color inspiration: gold / honey (kind influence).
  • Body relation: throat (expression) + heart (tone).
Daily Practice
  • Before speaking: “Is it true? Is it necessary? Is it kind?”
  • One repair conversation per week (apology or clarification).
  • Write 3 affirmations you can honestly embody.
Eshu Accountability
  • No gossip. No “hidden insults.”
  • Say what you mean, mean what you say.
  • Accept outcomes of your words.
4
Re-membering the Self
Auset: restore Ausar Ileke: Ogun (paths) Eshu: road opens with effort
Public Focus
  • Theme: rebuild what is broken through steady action.
  • Color inspiration: green / black (growth + discipline).
  • Season: spring→summer threshold (renewal into strength).
Daily Practice
  • 15 minutes physical discipline (walk / stretch / strength).
  • Choose one “unfinished thing” and complete it this week.
  • Write: “What do I need to repair in my life?”
Eshu Accountability
  • No excuses. Track effort.
  • Stop blaming “the road.” Become the road-maker.
  • Show consistency; then watch openings appear.
5
Spiritual Motherhood
Auset: nurture Ileke: Yemọja (healing) Eshu: emotional honesty
Public Focus
  • Theme: care for the inner life; heal emotional memory.
  • Color inspiration: blue / white (calm + cleansing).
  • Body relation: breath + hydration + rest.
Daily Practice
  • Water intention: drink water mindfully (30–60 seconds).
  • Journal: “What feeling needs compassion, not judgment?”
  • One nurturing act (self-care or care for others).
Eshu Accountability
  • No emotional manipulation.
  • Speak needs directly (without blame).
  • Choose healing over reaction.
6
Protection of the Awakened Will
Auset: guardianship Ileke: Oya (change) Eshu: decisive movement
Public Focus
  • Theme: protect your growth with boundaries.
  • Color inspiration: purple / burgundy (transformative power).
  • Season: autumn (release + reorientation).
Daily Practice
  • Write one boundary you will keep today.
  • Remove one source of stagnation (small but real).
  • Breath practice: 4-4-6 breathing (3 rounds).
Eshu Accountability
  • No “half-truths.” Boundaries require clarity.
  • Do not chase what disrespects you.
  • Choose the road that honors your destiny.
7
Veiled Wisdom & Ancestral Memory
Auset: mysteries Ileke: Egungun (lineage) Eshu: interpretation of signs
Public Focus
  • Theme: learn by observation; respect lineage and elders.
  • Color inspiration: earth tones / multi (heritage + community).
  • Body relation: posture + patience + listening.
Daily Practice
  • Record a dream or symbol (even one sentence).
  • Write: “What lesson keeps repeating in my life?”
  • Learn one story from family history (ask or reflect).
Eshu Accountability
  • Don’t rush to “decode” everything. Be patient with meaning.
  • No inventing facts. Keep interpretation humble.
  • Honor elders, ancestors, and the truth.
8
Cosmic Order & Righteous Authority
Auset: Maat through love Ileke: Shango (truth) Eshu: consequence & justice
Public Focus
  • Theme: live what you know; embody truth and fairness.
  • Color inspiration: red / white (power + integrity).
  • Season: summer (full expression of character).
Daily Practice
  • Truth inventory: one honest correction daily.
  • Write: “Where must I be more consistent?”
  • Offer service: one helpful act with no publicity.
Eshu Accountability
  • Truth without cruelty; firmness without ego.
  • Accept feedback. Correct course quickly.
  • Justice begins with self-discipline.
Public Completion (Suggested)
  • Write a one-page reflection: “How my character changed in 8 weeks.”
  • Choose one principle to repeat for 30 days.
  • If you want priest-guided work, request a private consultation.
